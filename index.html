ACCESSIBILITY

Visual & interaction preferences

Keyboard access, visible focus, screen-reader announcements, labeled controls, and a text alternative to the map are provided. WCAG 2.2 AA is the design target; formal auditing is pending.

RECOGNITION CONTROLS

Field detection tuning

Colors locate possible sign regions; optional COCO detection finds Stop signs. MobileCLIP classifies crops, with two matching checks before automatic logging. Neither model is calibrated for Omani signs.
