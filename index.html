ACCESSIBILITY

Visual & interaction preferences

Keyboard access, visible focus, screen-reader announcements, labeled controls, and a text alternative to the map are provided. WCAG 2.2 AA is the design target; formal auditing is pending.

RECOGNITION CONTROLS

Field detection tuning

Color and geometry locate candidates in live frames; on-device visual similarity suggests one of 25 signs. It is NOT a calibrated object-detection model and may miss or mislabel signs.

PRIVACY & DATA

Your data remains yours

Scan records stay in this device's browser storage; there is no login, backend, or syncing. The browser downloads the AI model and map tiles from third parties. Location access is optional.
